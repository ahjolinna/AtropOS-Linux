# Wallpaper suggestions

Studies for an AtropOS wallpaper. `05-cut.jpg` is now AtropOS 04 and `11-water.jpg` is now AtropOS 03 in `atropos-wallpapers`. The global theme default is AtropOS 02, which is not one of these studies.

The palette is that wallpaper’s: charcoal, silver, bone white, and crimson. The picture style follows the Elfen Lied opening, which is a Gustav Klimt frieze: flat ornament, lilies, and a quiet sacred mood. The figure is Atropos, the fate who cuts the thread. That is the name of the OS, and it is also what the OS does: one clean cut, then the system stays as it is. She is an original figure. She is not Lucy, and these are not copies of the Klimt paintings or of the study you already had.

- `01-throne.jpg` — she sits on a black throne and cuts one red thread. Closest to a full desktop painting.
- `02-profile.jpg` — she stands at the right. The left side is mostly empty, so icons have room.
- `03-fates.jpg` — spinner and measurer faded at the sides, Atropos clear in the center.
- `04-shears.jpg` — only the hands, the shears, a silver crown, a lily, and rose petals. The quietest of the four.
- `05-cut.jpg` — she stands at the left just after the cut. The right side is mostly empty.
- `06-horizon.jpg` — one red thread crosses the frame, and she cuts it at the center. The darkest and most open.
- `07-window.jpg` — she stands in a lily arch. The shears are oversized, and one blade is still long.
- `08-crown.jpg` — she faces forward in a silver spiky crown. A few border panels still have letter-like marks.

`01` through `08` follow the Elfen Lied opening, a Gustav Klimt frieze. Gold there is only thin linework.

`09` through `12` follow Akseli Gallen-Kallela’s Kalevala paintings: heavy outlines, a black lake, pines, and granite. They are not copies of the Aino triptych, Lemminkäinen’s Mother, or The Defence of the Sampo.

- `09-shore.jpg` — she stands on the shore and cuts a thread that runs out over the lake.
- `10-cliff.jpg` — she stands on a cliff at the left. The thread crosses an open sky.
- `11-water.jpg` — she kneels between two walls of spruce and cuts the thread over black water. The spruces picked up a little green.
- `12-birch.jpg` — she sits on a pale rock under a birch, the cut thread beside her, the lake mirroring her.

`13` through `16` keep the woman from `06-horizon.jpg` and change only the place around her.

- `13-lake.jpg` — the same woman on a black lakeshore, the thread still across the frame.
- `14-hall.jpg` — the same woman in a dark hall, a gothic window behind her, rose petals on the floor.
- `15-lily.jpg` — the same woman at the right, one lily on the floor, the left side empty.
- `16-rock.jpg` — the same woman seated on a pale rock in black water, a birch behind her, the cut thread on the stone.
